How to use EION Harmony

Watch the quick guide, then follow the steps below.

  1. Drink 1-2 glasses of water before blowing the body.
  2. Focus on the affected area or use for 2-3 minutes per area.
  3. Keep about 3 inches away from the skin and blow in a counter-clockwise direction.
  4. Follow the body guide: palms, soles, around the knees, groin, armpit, neck, upper back, head and butt.
  5. Drink 1-2 glasses of water after blowing the body.

Caution: users with high blood pressure, please take care with the head step.

EION Harmony is also great on the face and all over the body or limbs that are in pain.
